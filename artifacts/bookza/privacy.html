Qwickly is a booking and client-management platform for South African service businesses. Protecting the personal information entrusted to us — by the businesses that use Qwickly and by their clients — is central to how we build the product. This policy describes our practices in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA).

1. Introduction

[Placeholder] This Privacy Policy applies to the Qwickly website, dashboard, public booking pages, and related services (together, the "Service"). By using the Service you acknowledge the practices described here.

[Placeholder] Where a business uses Qwickly to manage its own clients, that business is the responsible party (data controller) for its client data, and Qwickly acts as an operator (processor) on its behalf. This distinction is explained further below.

2. Who we are

[Placeholder] Qwickly is operated by [legal entity name], registered in South Africa [registration number]. Our registered address is [address]. Our Information Officer can be reached at [privacy@qwickly.co.za].

3. Information we collect

[Placeholder] We collect the following categories of information:

Information businesses provide

  • Account and contact details (name, email, phone number, business name).
  • Business configuration (services, staff, operating hours, pricing).
  • Billing and subscription information.

Client information entered by businesses

  • Client names, phone numbers, and email addresses.
  • Booking history, notes, and payment records.

Information collected automatically

  • Device, browser, and usage data.
  • Log data and approximate location derived from IP address.

4. How we use information

[Placeholder] We use personal information to provide, maintain, and improve the Service, including to:

  • Create and manage bookings, reminders, and client records.
  • Send transactional messages and notifications on a business's behalf.
  • Process payments and manage subscriptions.
  • Provide support, security, and fraud prevention.
  • Understand product usage and improve features.

5. WhatsApp & messaging data

[Placeholder] Qwickly connects to WhatsApp to send reminders, confirmations, campaigns, and AI-assisted replies. When a business connects WhatsApp, we process the message content and contact details needed to deliver these features.

[Placeholder] WhatsApp is a third-party service governed by its own terms and privacy policy. Businesses are responsible for ensuring they have a lawful basis to message their clients.

6. Sharing & service providers

[Placeholder] We do not sell personal information. We share it only with the service providers (operators) that help us run Qwickly, such as hosting, database, payment, messaging, and AI providers, and only as needed to deliver the Service. A current list of sub-processors is available on request.

7. Data retention

[Placeholder] We retain personal information for as long as an account is active or as needed to provide the Service, and thereafter only as required to meet legal, accounting, or reporting obligations. Businesses can request deletion of their data as described below.

8. Security

[Placeholder] We use technical and organisational measures — including encryption in transit, access controls, and regular reviews — to protect personal information. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your rights (POPIA)

[Placeholder] Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information, to object to certain processing, and to lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa.

  • Access a copy of the information we hold about you.
  • Request correction or deletion of that information.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.

[Placeholder] To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below. Clients of a business should contact that business directly, as it is the responsible party for their data.

10. Cookies & tracking

[Placeholder] We use cookies and similar technologies to keep you signed in, remember preferences, and understand how the Service is used. You can control cookies through your browser settings. A separate cookie notice will be provided where required.

11. International transfers

[Placeholder] Some of our service providers may process information outside South Africa. Where this happens, we take steps to ensure the information receives an adequate level of protection consistent with POPIA.

12. Children's privacy

[Placeholder] The Service is intended for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children except where a business records a minor as a client with appropriate consent.

13. Changes to this policy

[Placeholder] We may update this policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date and, where appropriate, notify you through the Service.

14. Contact us

[Placeholder] For any privacy questions or to exercise your rights, contact our Information Officer at privacy@qwickly.co.za or visit our contact page.
