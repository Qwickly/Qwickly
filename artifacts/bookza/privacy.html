Privacy Policy

This policy explains what personal information Qwickly collects, how we use it, and the choices and rights you have. It covers both the businesses that use Qwickly and the clients they serve.

Qwickly is a booking, client-management, messaging, payment-tracking, campaign, analytics, and AI-assisted admin platform for service businesses. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, retain, and protect personal information when Businesses and Clients use Qwickly. It is written with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA) in mind.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to Qwickly's website, dashboard, public booking pages, APIs, notifications, messaging features, campaigns, AI-assisted tools, and related services (together, the "Service"). It should be read together with our Terms of Service.

When a Business uses Qwickly to manage its own Clients, the Business generally decides why and how Client personal information is processed. In POPIA language, the Business is usually the responsible party for Client information, and Qwickly acts as an operator that processes that information on the Business's behalf. For information about our own users, billing, security, website, and support operations, Qwickly acts as the responsible party.

2. Who we are

Qwickly provides software tools for service businesses to manage bookings, clients, staff, services, payment records, reminders, WhatsApp inbox workflows, campaigns, emails, analytics, and AI-assisted booking admin.

For privacy questions, data requests, or Information Officer enquiries, contact us at privacy@qwickly.co.za or through our contact page. If you are a Client of a Business that uses Qwickly, you should usually contact that Business first because it controls your booking and client record.

3. Information we collect

The information we collect depends on how you use Qwickly.

Account, business, and user information

  • name, email address, phone number, password/authentication data, user ID, business name, business description, business contact details, and account settings;
  • business operating hours, services, service prices, service duration, deposits, booking rules, cancellation and rescheduling policies, staff profiles, staff roles, staff availability, team members, and notification settings;
  • subscription plan, add-ons, staff seats, credit balances, credit purchases, billing status, invoice/payment history, and payment-provider references.

Client, booking, and payment information

  • Client names, phone numbers, email addresses, notes, tags/status, preferences, visit history, no-show counts, and communication context;
  • bookings, requested services, assigned staff, dates and times, booking status, cancellation/reschedule details, totals, deposits, payment status, and manage-booking tokens;
  • payment records such as amount, method, type, status, paid date, provider reference, and provider payloads needed to reconcile payments.

Messaging, campaigns, and AI information

  • WhatsApp connection status, session records, QR/connection information, phone identifiers, conversation metadata, inbound messages, message history, and message content where required to provide inbox and AI features;
  • AI drafts, suggested replies, inbound message text, booking-intent decisions, proposed actions, business context used for replies, FAQs, working hours, services, staff, bookings, and deposit settings;
  • campaign names, subjects, messages, content type, selected audiences, recipient records, delivery/sending status, engagement records, usage counters, and campaign images;
  • email domain information, DNS verification records, sender details, reply-to addresses, and email delivery data.

Files, images, and public booking pages

  • booking page logos, cover images, campaign images, public booking page slugs, page titles, welcome messages, brand colours, and other content uploaded or published through the Service.

Website, device, and support information

  • IP address, browser, device, operating system, approximate location, pages viewed, referral URLs, log data, errors, security events, and product usage events;
  • messages you send through contact forms, support requests, feedback, and other communications with us.

4. How we use information

We use personal information for the following purposes:

  • to create accounts, authenticate users, maintain sessions, and secure access;
  • to create, manage, update, cancel, reschedule, and display bookings;
  • to manage Clients, services, staff, working hours, payment rules, deposits, business settings, and public booking pages;
  • to send or queue transactional emails, booking confirmations, reminders, cancellation notices, rescheduling alerts, daily staff summaries, and other notifications configured by a Business;
  • to manage WhatsApp inbox workflows, existing-inbox campaigns, AI-assisted replies, AI drafts, approval flows, and booking actions requested through conversations;
  • to process subscription billing, add-ons, credit balances, top-ups, PayFast payment flows, invoices, payment records, and fraud or misuse prevention;
  • to send email campaigns where configured by a Business and where credits/limits allow;
  • to provide analytics, dashboards, activity logs, reporting, usage limits, and performance insights;
  • to provide customer support, troubleshoot bugs, monitor delivery failures, improve reliability, and develop new features;
  • to comply with law, enforce our Terms, protect rights and safety, investigate misuse, and maintain audit/security records.

Where Qwickly acts as an operator for a Business, we process Client information according to the Business's instructions and the functionality the Business enables, unless law requires otherwise.

5. WhatsApp, email & AI data

When a Business connects WhatsApp or uses AI-assisted booking features, Qwickly may process WhatsApp identifiers, session data, message metadata, message content, Client phone numbers, conversation history, business context, services, staff, booking availability, payment/deposit settings, FAQs, and prior booking records so the assistant can draft replies, detect intent, and perform or suggest booking actions.

AI providers may process prompts and context that include message text, Client names or phone numbers, booking details, service details, staff details, and business settings where needed to generate a response or decision. We aim to send only the context needed for the requested feature, but Businesses should avoid placing unnecessary sensitive information in messages, notes, FAQs, or prompts.

Email features may share recipient addresses, sender details, reply-to addresses, subject lines, message content, and delivery metadata with our email provider. Payment features may share payment fields and references with payment providers. WhatsApp/Meta features are also subject to Meta's own terms and privacy practices.

Businesses are responsible for ensuring they have a lawful basis to message Clients and for honouring opt-outs, marketing restrictions, and platform rules.

6. Sharing & service providers

We do not sell personal information. We share personal information only where needed to operate Qwickly, provide requested features, comply with law, or protect the Service.

Categories of recipients may include:

  • hosting, database, authentication, and storage providers, including Supabase and infrastructure providers used to run the Service;
  • payment providers, including PayFast or other providers used for payment flows, billing, and payment notifications;
  • messaging and communication providers, including WhatsApp/Meta, SendGrid, email domain/DNS providers, and other delivery providers;
  • AI providers used to generate AI-assisted replies, decisions, summaries, or booking actions;
  • analytics, logging, monitoring, and security providers used to understand usage, detect errors, prevent abuse, and protect the Service;
  • professional advisers, authorities, or courts where needed for legal, tax, accounting, dispute, compliance, or regulatory reasons;
  • successors or acquirers if Qwickly is involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, restructuring, or sale of assets, subject to appropriate confidentiality and legal safeguards.

We require service providers to process personal information only for authorised purposes and to protect it with appropriate safeguards.

7. Data retention

We keep personal information for as long as needed to provide the Service, maintain accurate business and booking records, comply with legal/accounting/tax obligations, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, prevent abuse, and preserve security logs.

Different records may have different retention periods. For example, active booking, Client, message, campaign, and payment records are generally kept while a Business account is active; billing and audit records may be retained longer where legally required; temporary logs and cached data may be retained for shorter periods; backups may persist for a limited period before being overwritten or deleted.

If a Business deletes its account or asks us to delete Customer Data, we will take reasonable steps to delete or de-identify the data unless we need to retain it for legal, security, billing, dispute, or compliance reasons. Clients should direct deletion requests to the Business that controls their record.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards designed to protect personal information against loss, misuse, unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, and destruction. These safeguards may include authentication, access controls, encrypted connections, role-based access, service-role separation, logging, provider security controls, and operational reviews.

No online system is perfectly secure. You are responsible for protecting your login details, restricting team access, using trusted devices, managing connected WhatsApp accounts carefully, and notifying us promptly of suspected unauthorised access.

If we become aware of a security incident involving personal information, we will take steps required by applicable law, which may include notifying affected Businesses, data subjects, and/or the Information Regulator where required.

9. Your rights (POPIA)

Subject to applicable law, data subjects may have rights to:

  • know whether personal information is being collected or held;
  • request access to personal information;
  • request correction, deletion, or destruction of inaccurate, irrelevant, excessive, outdated, incomplete, misleading, or unlawfully obtained personal information;
  • object to processing in certain circumstances;
  • object to direct marketing by unsolicited electronic communications;
  • withdraw consent where processing is based on consent;
  • complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa.

If you are a Qwickly account user, contact us at privacy@qwickly.co.za. If you are a Client of a Business using Qwickly, contact that Business first because it is normally the responsible party for your booking and client record. We will support the Business where required to respond to lawful requests.

10. Cookies & tracking

We use cookies, local storage, and similar technologies to keep users signed in, remember interface settings, support security, maintain sessions, improve performance, and understand use of the Service. For example, the app may store authentication/session data through Supabase and may store small client-side caches such as interface state or conversation cache data.

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Blocking certain cookies or storage may prevent parts of the Service from working correctly.

11. International transfers

Some service providers, infrastructure, payment, messaging, email, AI, monitoring, or support systems may process information outside South Africa. Where personal information is transferred internationally, we take reasonable steps to use lawful transfer mechanisms and appropriate safeguards consistent with POPIA, such as contractual protections, provider security terms, or transfers necessary to perform the Service requested by the Business.

12. Children's privacy

Qwickly is designed for businesses and adult account users. We do not knowingly allow children to create Qwickly business accounts. A Business may record information about a minor as a Client, student, or appointment attendee where the Business has the required authority or consent from a parent, guardian, or other competent person. The Business is responsible for that lawful basis.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When changes are material, we will update the "Last updated" date and may notify users through the Service, by email, or by another reasonable method. Your continued use of the Service after an update means the updated policy applies going forward.

14. Contact us

For privacy questions, data requests, or Information Officer enquiries, contact privacy@qwickly.co.za or use our contact page.

You may also contact the Information Regulator of South Africa if you believe your rights under POPIA have been infringed.
