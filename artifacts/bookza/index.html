FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Quick answers about bookings, WhatsApp, AI-assisted workflows, campaigns, payments, and flexible Qwickly plans.

Can Qwickly handle online bookings?
Yes. Qwickly gives service businesses a public booking page where clients can choose services, staff, dates, and available time slots.
Does Qwickly send WhatsApp reminders?
Yes. Qwickly supports WhatsApp reminders and confirmations for clients already reachable in your inbox, with new-number sends coming soon.
Can I manage clients and staff?
Yes. You can manage client profiles, service history, staff schedules, services, durations, and booking rules from one workspace.
Does Qwickly support deposits?
Qwickly helps businesses track deposits and payment status so booking admin stays connected to the calendar and client record.
Can I send WhatsApp campaigns?
Yes. Campaigns help you message selected clients already in your WhatsApp inbox. Campaigns to brand-new WhatsApp numbers are coming soon.
How does the AI-assisted WhatsApp booking work?
Qwickly can help understand booking intent, ask for missing details, prepare booking replies, and keep review mode available before messages are sent.
Are all automation features live?
Some features are still coming soon, including email campaigns, SMS campaigns, review requests, advanced campaign analytics, richer billing tools, and more AI automation flows.
Can I build a custom plan?
Yes. Qwickly is moving toward flexible plans so businesses can choose the booking, WhatsApp, campaign, staff, payment, and workflow tools they need.
