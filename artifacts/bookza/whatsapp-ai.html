FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Quick answers about WhatsApp AI, review mode, automatic replies, booking actions, and the controls your team keeps.

Can the AI send replies automatically?
Yes, selected workflows can use Auto Mode when your services, settings, and preferred replies are configured. You can start with Review Mode first.
Can I approve replies before they are sent?
Yes. Review Mode lets Qwickly prepare the reply and booking action, then wait for your team to approve, edit, or dismiss it.
How does WhatsApp connect?
Qwickly connects to your business WhatsApp with a QR scan, then your team can manage the connection from the dashboard.
Can I disconnect WhatsApp?
Yes. You can disconnect WhatsApp anytime from your dashboard, so your business stays in control of the connection.
Can Qwickly create bookings from WhatsApp messages?
Qwickly can help detect booking intent, collect missing details, and prepare the booking action for review or approval.
Can it handle cancellations and reschedules?
Qwickly can help identify cancellation and reschedule requests, find the related booking, and prepare the next action.
Does the assistant know my services and staff?
It can use the services, staff, hours, and business details you configure in Qwickly when preparing replies and booking actions.
What happens if the client does not give enough details?
Qwickly can ask for missing information such as service, staff, date, time, or client details before preparing the action.
Can staff receive booking updates?
Yes. Qwickly can help keep staff and clients updated around prepared bookings, cancellations, and reschedules.
Is the knowledge base available?
The assistant can use configured business details for common questions. More knowledge-base and AI automation flows are planned as the product expands.
