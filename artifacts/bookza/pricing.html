FAQ

Pricing questions.

Quick answers about changing plans, WhatsApp usage, staff, campaigns, AI, custom workflows, and final pricing.

Can I change plans later?
Yes. For now, you start on Starter and add or remove optional add-ons from Billing. Teams will be introduced later for businesses that need multiple logins and richer permissions.
How do add-ons work?
Add-ons attach to your Starter subscription and renew as part of the same monthly billing total. Chatbot, analytics, and branded email are available during the trial so you can decide what to keep.
Are WhatsApp campaign tokens included?
No WhatsApp campaign tokens are included in Starter. You can buy WhatsApp token top-ups after you have an active plan.
Do I need to pay per staff member?
Starter includes one staff member. Extra staff seats are available as an add-on at R20 per staff member per month.
Are campaigns included?
Starter includes 100 marketing emails per month. Extra marketing email and WhatsApp token top-ups are bought separately once you have a plan.
Is WhatsApp AI included?
The WhatsApp Chatbot is included during the 14-day trial. After the trial, it is an optional R200/month add-on.
When is Teams coming?
Teams is planned for businesses that need multiple user logins, role permissions, team notifications, and larger operating workflows. It is not available to buy yet.
Are the prices final?
The live Starter and add-on prices shown here match the current billing model. Teams pricing is still to be announced.
