FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Quick answers about WhatsApp campaign audiences, images, send limits, booking links, and scheduling.

Can I choose which clients receive a campaign?
Yes. Qwickly campaigns are designed around selected client lists and targeted audiences.
Can I send campaigns with images?
Yes. Campaigns can include visual content so promotions and announcements feel more polished.
How many messages can I send per day?
The campaign page is positioned around up to 2,000 WhatsApp messages per day, subject to your setup and usage limits.
Can I include a booking link?
Yes. Campaign messages can point clients back to your online booking flow so interest can turn into bookings.
Can I schedule campaigns?
Yes. Campaigns can be prepared and scheduled, or sent when your campaign is ready.
Is this cheaper than SMS campaign tools?
Qwickly is positioned around lower-cost WhatsApp outreach for client lists, especially compared with many SMS-heavy campaign workflows.
Can I send to inactive clients?
Yes. Reactivation campaigns are one of the main use cases, helping you reach clients who have not booked recently.
Can I preview a campaign before sending?
Yes. The campaign builder includes a preview step so you can check the message, image, and booking link before sending.
