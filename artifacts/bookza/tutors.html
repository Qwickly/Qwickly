FAQ

Common questions.

Quick answers about WhatsApp bookings, online booking, tutors, classes, payments, reminders, and student details.

Can parents and students book through WhatsApp?
Yes. Qwickly helps parents and students request lessons through WhatsApp while your business manages bookings, availability, confirmations, and student details from one dashboard.
Can lessons also be booked online?
Yes. Qwickly gives your business an online booking page that parents and students can use to view classes, choose available times, and request or confirm lessons.
Can I review bookings before they are confirmed?
Yes. Qwickly includes review mode so you can check AI-assisted booking replies or booking actions before anything is confirmed or sent to a parent.
Can I manage different tutors and classes?
Yes. You can organise bookings by class, tutor, time, and availability, making it easier to manage teams, calendars, and any lesson changes.
Can Qwickly help reduce missed lessons?
Yes. Qwickly supports confirmations and reminders for contacts already reachable in your WhatsApp inbox, with new-number sends coming soon.
Can I take deposits or payments for lessons?
Yes. Deposits or payments can be tracked for selected classes or lesson types to help protect your time and reduce unpaid cancellations or no-shows.
Does Qwickly store student details and history?
Yes. Student profiles can include contact details, booking history, notes, preferences, reminder activity, and other useful context for future lessons.
Do I need a website to use Qwickly?
No. Qwickly can provide an online booking page, and parents and students can also interact through WhatsApp. You can link your booking page from your website, Instagram, Facebook, Google profile, or WhatsApp profile.
