Can I customize the reminder messages?
Yes. Reminder templates support variables like client name, service, and booking date and time.
Can I turn off specific reminders?
Yes. Confirmation, 24-hour, 2-hour, and follow-up reminders can each be toggled on or off.
Do clients need to install anything?
No. Reminders are sent to WhatsApp, which most clients already have installed.
Is this different from WhatsApp AI?
Yes. Reminders are automatic one-way and two-way messages. WhatsApp AI adds intent detection and booking actions on top.
Can my team see reminder history?
Yes. Sent reminders and client replies stay visible in your WhatsApp conversations.
