Yes. Existing client details can be brought into Qwickly so history starts from day one.

Yes. Notes, preferences, and important details stay attached to the client record.

No. Client profiles are for your team. Clients interact through booking and WhatsApp only.

Yes. You can message a client from their profile using the same WhatsApp connection.
