FAQ

Contact questions.

Quick answers before you send us a message.

Can I ask about custom workflows?
Yes. Tell us about your booking rules, automations, reporting needs, or business-specific workflow.
Can you help me choose a plan?
Yes. Share your team size, booking volume, WhatsApp needs, and campaign goals so we can point you in the right direction.
Can I use Qwickly if I mainly take bookings through WhatsApp?
Yes. Qwickly is designed for service businesses that already rely heavily on WhatsApp for bookings and client communication.
Is WhatsApp AI available on all plans?
WhatsApp AI availability can depend on your plan and setup. Ask us and we will explain the best fit.
Can you help migrate from my current booking system?
We can talk through your current setup and what would be needed to move services, clients, staff, and booking workflows into Qwickly.
Do I need technical knowledge to set up Qwickly?
No. Qwickly is being built for everyday service teams, with settings and workflows that should be clear to manage.
How quickly do you respond?
Response time may vary while Qwickly is still early, but we aim to reply as soon as possible.
Is the contact form connected yet?
The form is currently a static mailto form. Backend submission still needs to be connected before production use.
