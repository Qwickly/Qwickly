Can I see every staff member's schedule at once?
Yes. The week view shows every staff member's bookings together, color-coded by status.
Can clients see this calendar?
No. This is your internal admin view. Clients use your public online booking page instead.
Can I create a booking manually?
Yes. Your team can add a booking directly from the calendar for walk-ins or phone requests.
Does it sync with online bookings?
Yes. Bookings made through your public booking page appear on this calendar automatically.
Can I block off unavailable time?
Yes. Blocked time and staff schedules keep the calendar accurate for your whole team.
