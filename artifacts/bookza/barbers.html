FAQ

Common questions.

Quick answers about WhatsApp bookings, online booking, staff, services, deposits, reminders, and client details.

Can clients book through WhatsApp?
Yes. Qwickly helps clients request appointments through WhatsApp while your business manages bookings, availability, confirmations, and client details from one dashboard.
Can clients also book online?
Yes. Qwickly gives your business an online booking page that clients can use to view services, choose available times, and request or confirm appointments.
Can I review bookings before they are confirmed?
Yes. Qwickly includes review mode so you can check AI-assisted booking replies or booking actions before anything is confirmed or sent to a client.
Can I manage different barbers and chairs?
Yes. You can organise bookings by service, barber, chair, time, and availability, making it easier to manage teams, calendars, and appointment changes.
Can Qwickly help reduce no-shows?
Yes. Qwickly supports booking confirmations and automatic reminders, helping clients remember their appointments and reducing last-minute confusion.
Can I take deposits for bookings?
Yes. Deposits can be used for selected services or appointment types to help protect your chair time and reduce unpaid cancellations or no-shows.
Does Qwickly store client details and history?
Yes. Client profiles can include contact details, booking history, notes, preferences, reminder activity, and other useful context for future appointments.
Do I need a website to use Qwickly?
No. Qwickly can provide an online booking page, and clients can also interact through WhatsApp. You can link your booking page from your website, Instagram, Facebook, Google profile, or WhatsApp profile.
