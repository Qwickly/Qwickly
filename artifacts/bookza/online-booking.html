Business control

Your booking page follows your rules.

Clients get a simple booking experience — you keep control of the rules behind every appointment.

Advance booking limits

Decide how far ahead — and how last-minute — clients can book, so your day never fills with surprises.

Cancellation windows

Set the cut-off for cancellations, so a last-minute change doesn't quietly cost you the slot.

Reschedule rules

Let clients move their own appointment — but only up to the point that still works for your team.

Deposits & no-show cover

Ask for a deposit on the services that hurt most when a client doesn't show, per service and only where you need it.

No double-bookings

The moment a client requests a time, that slot is held — so two people can never grab the same appointment.

Staff availability

Working hours, breaks, days off, and which staff perform each service — all respected before a slot is offered.

Approval mode

Auto-confirm the bookings you trust, and hold the rest for a quick team review before they land.

FAQ

Online booking questions.

Quick answers about booking pages, services, staff availability, deposits, reminders, and campaigns.

Can clients book online?
Yes. Clients can use your Qwickly booking page to choose a service, staff member, date, and available time.
Can I add multiple services?
Yes. You can create services with names, durations, prices, staff assignments, and online visibility rules.
Can I set staff availability?
Yes. Staff availability can be used to show clients only the slots that can actually be booked.
Can I choose which services appear online?
Yes. Keep internal services private and only publish the services you want clients to book themselves.
Can clients book from WhatsApp or Instagram?
Yes. Share the same booking link in WhatsApp chats, Instagram bios, websites, and campaigns.
Can I take deposits?
Qwickly supports deposit and payment rules so you can protect your time and reduce no-shows.
Can clients cancel or reschedule?
Booking workflows can support cancellation and reschedule rules depending on how your business wants to manage changes.
Do clients get reminders?
Yes. Online bookings can work with Qwickly reminders so clients stay updated before appointments.
Can I block off unavailable times?
Yes. Blocked times and staff schedules help keep the booking page accurate.
Can I use bookings with campaigns?
Yes. Campaigns can send clients back to your booking link so promotions and reactivation messages lead to appointments.
